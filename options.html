Data

Cached lookups
Checking…

Film links are remembered for 7 days so pages load without re-asking Plex. The library index behind the poster badges is a list of your Plex titles, rebuilt every 30 minutes and dropped when the browser closes. Clear both if something points somewhere stale, or after changing servers. Everything ReelHop stores stays in this browser.

ReelHop is unofficial and not affiliated with Letterboxd, IMDb, Plex, or Radarr.
